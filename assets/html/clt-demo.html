How to Use:

  • Flips per Experiment: Number of coin flips in each trial
  • Number of Experiments: How many times to repeat the experiment
  • Coin Bias: Probability of getting heads (0.5 = fair coin)

Observe: As you increase the number of flips, the distribution becomes more normal (bell-shaped), demonstrating the Central Limit Theorem!
